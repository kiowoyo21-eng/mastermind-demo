WHEN TO HAVE IT CHECKED

Common reasons to book

  • Kalampag, clunking, squeaking, or knocking over bumps
  • Vehicle pulling, wandering, or feeling unstable
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Excessive bounce or harsh ride
  • Loose, heavy, or inconsistent steering feel

WHAT THE PROCESS MAY INCLUDE

Inspection & diagnosis

  • Suspension joints, bushings, mounts, and dampers
  • Steering linkage and related components
  • Visible tire wear patterns
  • Wheel movement and play where applicable
  • Condition-based recommendation before parts replacement

FAQ

Useful before
you visit.

These answers describe the service approach, not a diagnosis for a specific vehicle. The actual recommendation depends on inspection.

Does every suspension noise mean the shock absorber is bad?

No. Similar noises can come from bushings, links, mounts, joints, loose hardware, or other chassis components.

Can alignment solve pulling by itself?

Sometimes, but pulling may also involve tire condition, suspension wear, brake drag, or other factors. The cause should be checked first.

Should I have a noise checked even if the car still drives normally?

Yes. A developing chassis issue is often easier to address when inspected before it becomes severe.
