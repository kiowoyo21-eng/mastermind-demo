WHEN TO HAVE IT CHECKED

Common reasons to book

  • Unusual engine noise, vibration, or loss of power

you visit.

These answers describe the service approach, not a diagnosis for a specific vehicle. The actual recommendation depends on inspection.

Can you diagnose an engine concern before replacing parts?

That is the goal. Parts replacement should follow evidence from inspection and testing rather than begin with a guess.

Should I stop driving if there is a warning light or severe noise?

If the vehicle is overheating, losing oil pressure, making severe mechanical noise, or behaving unsafely, avoid continued driving and arrange appropriate assistance.

Can I include videos or previous repair notes?

Yes. Add the details to your visit request and bring any previous diagnostic or repair documentation you have.
