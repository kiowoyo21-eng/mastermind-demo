SERVICE PRINCIPLE

Know the cause before the repair. Schedule a visit with the exact symptom, vehicle details, and preferred time so the team can prepare the right first step.

WHEN TO HAVE IT CHECKED

Common reasons to book

  • Warning lights or recurring fault codes
  • Hard starting, stalling, or rough running
  • Loss of power or unusual vehicle behavior
  • Electrical or sensor-related concerns
  • A previous repair that did not solve the original issue

WHAT THE PROCESS MAY INCLUDE

Inspection & diagnosis

  • Customer concern and vehicle history
  • Visual and functional checks relevant to the symptom
  • Diagnostic scan data and stored fault information
  • Targeted testing and measurements where appropriate
  • A clear explanation of findings and recommended next step

FAQ

Useful before
you visit.

These answers describe the service approach, not a diagnosis for a specific vehicle. The actual recommendation depends on inspection.

Does a fault code tell you which part to replace?

Not by itself. A code points to a system or condition. Proper diagnosis may still require inspection, live data, measurements, and component or circuit testing.

Will you explain the findings before repair?

The process is designed around explaining what was found and what the next practical step is before work proceeds.

Can I request diagnostics even if another shop already checked the car?

Yes. Include any previous findings, repair history, or scan results in your visit request so the team has useful context.
