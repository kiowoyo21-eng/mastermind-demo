Preventive Maintenance
Mandaluyong.

Routine service and inspection help preserve performance, identify developing wear, and make upcoming maintenance easier to plan.

SERVICE PRINCIPLE

Plan care before wear becomes a breakdown. Request a visit with the exact symptom, vehicle details, and preferred schedule so the team can prepare the right first step.

WHEN TO HAVE IT CHECKED

Common reasons to book

  • Your vehicle is due by mileage or time interval
  • Fluids, filters, brakes, or underchassis items need checking
  • You are preparing for a long drive
  • You recently acquired a used vehicle and want a baseline inspection
  • You want to plan maintenance rather than react to failures

WHAT THE PROCESS MAY INCLUDE

Inspection & diagnosis

  • Vehicle mileage, age, and recent service history
  • Fluids, filters, belts, hoses, and visible wear items
  • Brake, tire, suspension, and underbody condition where relevant
  • Battery and basic electrical health checks where applicable
  • Prioritized recommendations based on current condition

FAQ

Useful before
you visit.

These answers describe the service approach, not a diagnosis for a specific vehicle. The actual recommendation depends on inspection.

Do you follow the vehicle’s maintenance schedule?

Bring the vehicle manual or recent service record if available. Recommended work should account for the manufacturer schedule, current mileage, and actual vehicle condition.

Can you prioritize what needs to be done first?

Yes. A useful maintenance plan separates immediate needs from items that can be monitored or scheduled later.

Can I request a general check before a road trip?

Yes. Describe the trip and any current concerns in the visit request so the inspection can focus on practical readiness items.
