THE MASTERMIND STANDARD

Understand first. Recommend second. The website experience is designed around clarity before repair—not a list of parts before the cause is understood.

01 — PRINCIPLES

What the standard
is built around.

The goal is a service conversation that gives the customer enough information to understand the concern and decide what happens next.

DIAGNOSIS

Find the cause.

Start with the symptom, history, inspection, scan data, testing, and relevant measurements rather than guessing which component to replace.

CLARITY

Explain the finding.

Organize the information around what was found, why it matters, and what the next practical step may be.

AUTHORIZATION

Decide before work proceeds.

Scheduling or consultation starts the conversation. Repair decisions should follow a clear recommendation and customer authorization.

VERIFICATION

Check the result.

Verification should match the repair performed—whether that means a functional check, scan, measurement, inspection, or road test.

02 — WHAT TO EXPECT

A clearer path
from symptom to solution.

Every vehicle concern is different, but the decision-making sequence should remain easy to understand.

01

Describe

Share the vehicle, symptom, warning light, noise, recent work, and when the concern happens.

02

Inspect

The first checks are guided by the actual complaint rather than a generic parts list.

03

Understand

Findings are translated into a clear recommendation so the next step makes sense.

04

Proceed

Work moves forward after the recommendation is understood and authorized.
