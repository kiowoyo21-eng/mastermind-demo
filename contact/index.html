02 — BEFORE YOU MESSAGE

A little context
helps the first conversation.

You do not need to diagnose the vehicle yourself. A clear description of what you notice is enough to begin.

What vehicle details should I send?

Year, make, model, and the specific symptom or warning light are a useful starting point.

Is a preferred date automatically confirmed?

No. The scheduling flow creates a visit request. Your preferred schedule is confirmed when the Mastermind team replies with availability.

What if I do not know which service to choose?

Use Consult Now or select “Not sure yet” on the scheduling form and describe the symptom.

How can I confirm payment methods or warranty coverage?

Ask the team before work begins so the available payment options and any service-specific coverage can be confirmed for your job.

Where can I check the latest shop hours?

Use the Mastermind Autoworks Google Maps listing before travelling, especially on holidays or days with adjusted operating hours.
